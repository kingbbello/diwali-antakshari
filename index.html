Diwali Antakshari

YouTube usually refuses to play videos in a page opened straight from a file. In Terminal, go to the folder with this file and run python3 -m http.server 8000, then open http://localhost:8000/antakshari.html in Chrome.

Scoreboard

How a round works. The team on turn hears a 10 to 15 second clip with the screen hidden. When it stops, they sing what comes next. Then reveal the film scene to check. Suggested scoring: +1 for singing the next line, +1 for naming the film.

Keys: Space next step, R replay clip, ← → previous or next song.

Rehearse each song

Times are rough guesses. For each song, press Test clip and watch the stage. Use Set to now while it plays, or the − and + buttons, until the clip stops right before a line people will know. The reveal plays on from the pause point.
